About me

Connecting data, education, and human-centred AI.

I am a PhD candidate in Information Technology (Learning Analytics) at Monash University's Centre for Learning Analytics, and a Teaching Associate in its Faculty of Information Technology. I am also a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) at Universitas Teknokrat Indonesia.

My research asks how AI can help educators give feedback that students can act on. I have first-author publications in Computers & Education, Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, and Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education. I hold a Bachelor's and a Master's in Mathematics from Universitas Lampung.

Research expertise

Areas of focus

My work investigates how emerging technologies can strengthen, not replace, the relationships at the heart of education.

01

Learner-centred feedback

Operationalising Future Impact, Sensemaking, and Agency in feedback: actionable guidance, explanation, learner agency, and supportive interaction.

02

Generative AI & LLM evaluation

Designing and evaluating LLM-based feedback workflows across prompting conditions, and comparing AI-generated with teacher feedback on pedagogical and linguistic measures.

03

Educational NLP & datasets

Curating and annotating authentic feedback and student-work datasets; supervised and multilabel classification with BERT-based models and careful validation.

04

Learning analytics & outcomes

Statistical and text-analytic methods linking feedback, educator–AI interaction, and student revision to subsequent academic performance.

Doctoral research

Five connected studies

From mapping feedback, to automating its analysis, to how teachers and students use it.

  1. Study 1

    Mapping learner-centred feedback

    Analysed authentic higher-education feedback, its alignment with learner-centred components, and patterns across student groups.

    Published · Computers & Education, 2025
  2. Study 2

    Automating feedback analysis

    Developed machine learning and NLP approaches to classify learner-centred feedback; BERT-based classifiers reached Cohen's κ up to 0.956 and F1 up to 0.998. Implemented in PolyFeed.

    Published · Computers and Education: AI, 2024
  3. Study 3

    Human and LLM-generated feedback

    Compared 5,156 human- and LLM-generated feedback texts on 1,289 student proposals, across prompting strategies, on pedagogical and linguistic features.

    Manuscript in preparation
  4. Study 4

    Teacher–AI collaboration

    Investigated how educators use, revise, and trust AI-supported feedback in PolyFeed, including ethics and usability.

    Published · Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education, 2026
  5. Study 5

    Feedback and student outcomes

    Linked learner-centred feedback components to student revision actions and subsequent performance for 1,946 students across three semesters.

    Manuscript in preparation

Research output

PolyFeed: AI-supported feedback analytics

A feedback analytics system that includes the machine learning model I built in Study 2 to classify learner-centred feedback. It supported my research on automated feedback analysis and on educators' interaction with AI feedback tools.
