About me

Connecting data, education, and human-centred technology.

I am a PhD candidate at the Centre for Learning Analytics at Monash University and a lecturer in Informatics at Universitas Teknokrat Indonesia.

My research examines how AI and learning analytics can support educators in creating feedback that is more useful, personal, and responsive to learners’ needs.
