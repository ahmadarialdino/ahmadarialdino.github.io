Research focus

Learner-centred feedback

Operationalising Future Impact, Sensemaking, and Agency in feedback: actionable guidance, explanation, learner agency, and supportive interaction.

Generative AI & LLM evaluation

Designing and evaluating LLM feedback workflows across prompting conditions, and comparing AI-generated with teacher feedback on pedagogical and linguistic measures.

Educational NLP & datasets

Curating and annotating authentic feedback datasets; multilabel classification with BERT-based models and careful validation.

Learning analytics & outcomes

Linking feedback, educator–AI interaction, and student revision to subsequent academic performance.

Methods & tools: Python · R · SQL · BERT · scikit-learn · PyTorch · LLM APIs & prompt design · statistical modelling · qualitative thematic analysis · dataset annotation

Doctoral research

Five connected studies, from mapping feedback to automating its analysis to how teachers and students use it.

  1. 1

    Mapping learner-centred feedback

    Analysed authentic higher-education feedback, its alignment with learner-centred components, and patterns across student groups.

    Published · Computers & Education, 2025
  2. 2

    Automating feedback analysis

    Machine learning and NLP to classify learner-centred feedback; BERT-based classifiers reached Cohen's κ up to 0.956 and F1 up to 0.998. Implemented in PolyFeed.

    Published · Computers and Education: AI, 2024
  3. 3

    Human and LLM-generated feedback

    Compared 5,156 human- and LLM-generated feedback texts on 1,289 student proposals, across prompting strategies, on pedagogical and linguistic features.

    Under review
  4. 4

    Teacher–AI collaboration

    How educators use, revise, and trust AI-supported feedback in PolyFeed, including ethics and usability.

    Published · Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education, 2026
  5. 5

    Feedback and student outcomes

    Linked learner-centred feedback components to revision actions and subsequent performance for 1,946 students across three semesters.

    In preparation

PolyFeed, an AI-supported feedback analytics system, includes the classification model I built in Study 2. Meet the PolyFeed team.

Research agenda

From analysing feedback to improving it, with AI and teachers together.

Human–AI co-authored feedback

Design and test workflows where teachers and LLMs write feedback together, and measure whether it leads to better student revisions and learning than either alone.

Trustworthy evaluation of AI feedback

Evaluation frameworks that combine pedagogical quality, reliability, and fairness, so institutions can adopt AI feedback tools with evidence.

Feedback uptake at scale

Trace how students act on feedback over time across courses and institutions, moving towards longitudinal and quasi-experimental designs.
